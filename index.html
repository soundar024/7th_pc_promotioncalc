How it works

  1. One increment is calculated on the existing basic pay.
  2. The resulting amount is searched in the promoted Pay Level.
  3. The equal or next higher cell is selected as the fixed pay.
  4. The result and indicative increment date are displayed.

Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent to a server.
