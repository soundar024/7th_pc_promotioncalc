How the result was reached

Rule/date note: Annual increments are on 1 January or 1 July. The exact result can depend on the promotion date, qualifying service, the option exercised, and applicable Railway/CCS instructions.
Important: This is an indicative calculator, not an official sanction/fixation order. Verify the applicable Railway Board/Department of Expenditure instructions and the employee's service record before using the result officially.

Inputs

  • Existing DNI: the employee's current annual increment date before promotion.
  • Fix from promotion: applies the promotional fixation at the promotion stage.
  • Fix from DNI: models the deferred fixation route from the existing DNI.
