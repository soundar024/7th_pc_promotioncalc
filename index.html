Calculation

Increment-date rule: Annual increments are only on 1 January or 1 July. No calculation in this application can produce an annual increment date such as 1 August or 15 January.
Important: This is an indicative software calculator. Verify the employee's service record, promotion order, applicable Railway Board instructions and any special case before using the result for an official pay-fixation order.

Rules used

  • Rule 10: annual increment dates are 1 January and 1 July.
  • Rule 13: for promotion-date fixation, one increment is given in the lower Level and the employee is placed at the equal/next higher cell in the promoted Level.
  • DNI option: from promotion to DNI, the employee is placed at the next higher cell in the promoted Level; on DNI, pay is re-fixed after two increments in the lower Level and then placed at the equal/next higher cell in the promoted Level.

Pay matrix data includes Levels 1–18, including Level 13A. Level 13 reflects the revised 123100–215900 matrix.
